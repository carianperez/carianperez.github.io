roll call

cinnamon rolls, made in small batches around Tampa Bay

Roll Call started as a way to bring cinnamon rolls with real cream cheese icing to the neighborhood, not the shelf-stable kind. Every batch is baked to order, based in Riverview, with pickups and occasional pop-ups around the greater Tampa area.
